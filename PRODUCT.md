# AegisLand

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Present the existing independent UAV camera-reliability research, its retrospective evidence, working recorded-image prototype, and pending independent camera validation.

## Users

Inferred from the existing research site: visitors evaluating the research and readers exploring its reproducible evidence. No new audience or commercial claim is established by this redesign.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The homepage links to the working prototype, confidence audit, Failure Atlas, archived phases, reproduction instructions, and GitHub source. Its camera comparison uses the saved original-model records and existing six conditions. Phase 26 independent validation remains pending. Recorded-image and simulator evidence does not establish flight safety or real-world controller tuning.

## Brand Commitments

The user requested a website as close to Tesla's website as possible while retaining the existing information. Preserve the AegisLand name and existing research wording, metrics, links, and evidence boundaries. NASA media remains explicitly labeled as research context rather than AegisLand experiment data.
