Why this matters

Distribution shift attacked confidence before it erased the mean gain.

The frozen calibration looked strong on the earlier validation domain. Under new appearance + geometry shift, typical errors still improved — but the confidence envelope became too narrow and the difficult tail remained. That separates “better point estimates” from “reliable knowledge of when those estimates can be trusted.”

01 · Availability

Recovery helped, but not enough.

Miss rate improved from the paired Phase 9 baseline's 23.75% to 20.0%, still twice the preregistered ≤10% target.

02 · Tail risk

MAE hid the hard cases.

Average ambiguous errors fell strongly, while p95 barely moved. The remaining hard tail is now a first-class research target.

03 · Calibration

95% stopped meaning 95%.

Coverage fell to 84.3% lateral and 79.7% altitude. That is measurable overconfidence under shift, not a cosmetic metric miss.
