# Ethics and Safety Scope

This repository is a **simulation-first educational research project** about trustworthy autonomy.

It is not flight-control software, is not validated for real aircraft, and should not be used to operate a physical UAV.

The project intentionally focuses on:

- software simulation
- uncertainty measurement
- failure detection
- conservative supervisory decisions
- reproducibility
- transparent limitations

Any future physical validation should be performed only through an appropriate school/university lab or qualified adult-supervised environment with applicable safety rules and permissions.

The research goal is to understand **when autonomous perception should not be trusted**, not to maximize aggressive vehicle performance.
