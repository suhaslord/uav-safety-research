# Phase 26 secondary public-source screen — 10 October 2026

These sources were checked during the follow-up search for an independent
real-image landing-target collection. None is admitted to Phase 26.

| Source | Evidence | Gate disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Additional public-source sweep](phase26_public_source_search_sweep_2026-10-10.md) | Thirteen targeted queries screened AGH `drone_landing_static`, COMMTR real-video validation, Kaggle Landing Pad, DatasetUAVlanding, UDWA, and a TEKNOFEST mirror. | **No dataset admitted:** the leads fail target geometry or label scope, licensing, image-to-session provenance, or whole-session split requirements. The linked record gives the evidence for each; no images were downloaded and no inference was run. |
| [DDroneC-DB2](phase26_candidate_screen_dronedb2_2026-10-08.md) | The Dongguk study describes 10,642 images from DJI Phantom 4 landing videos and 1 m markers. | **Not admitted:** its published two-fold evaluation randomly splits images; source-video/session mapping and dataset-specific reuse terms are unavailable. |
| [UAVLandData](phase26_candidate_screen_uavlanddata_2026-10-10.md) | The paper describes 9,142 images from 13 hours of video across 24 landing scenarios. | **Not admitted:** the published image split is random, the paper inconsistently labels its 1,636-image evaluation partition, and per-image session provenance and exact file inventory are unavailable. |
| [TiHAN Marker Based Landing (MBL) Navigation Dataset](https://tihan.iith.ac.in/TiAND.html) | TiHAN lists 7,517 high-resolution images, 2D labels for a custom marker, and a 70/15/15 train/validation/test split across altitudes and weather. The associated [IEEE paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10990283/) describes high- and low-resolution data and ArUco-marker detection. | **Promising lead, not admitted:** the public page marks sensor details “Available Soon”; no MBL download or explicit reuse license is listed. The split is not stated to be grouped by capture session, and session IDs, negative-frame inventory, and target geometry are unavailable. Verify access terms and metadata before deciding whether it can support development or an independent test. See `docs/phase26_candidate_screen_tihan_mbl_2026-10-10.md`. |
| [LARD v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.09938v2) | Runway-corner detection for fixed-wing approach. The training set is synthetic; the test includes 103 real in-cockpit images from 38 runways at 36 airports. | **Out of scope:** runway ontology is not the KIOS landing-pad target. No pad-box mapping or session-level negative inventory is provided. |
| [LARD 2.0](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.26748v1) | Adds Google Earth, Bing Maps, ArcGIS, and flight-simulator sources for runway detection. | **Out of scope:** runway ontology and predominantly virtual/synthetic imagery. |
| [Autonomous landing with visual and IR fiducials](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.03806) | Reports 26 physical tests over four days and uses a visual pad made from three concentric AprilTag markers; sample landing videos are linked from the paper. | **Not a dataset release:** no frame-level annotated collection, row-to-session manifest, negative-frame inventory, or dataset reuse license was identified. Could be reconsidered only if the authors provide licensed source data and provenance. |
| [AirLab moving-vehicle ellipse dataset](https://theairlab.org/landing-on-vehicle/) | More than 1,500 frames for generic ellipse detection on a pattern placed on a moving vehicle. | **Out of scope:** not an annotated landing-pad-box dataset; session splits, negatives, and data license are not specified. |
| [Panoramic-sensor UAV landing thesis](https://trepo.tuni.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/150009/NguyenPhuoc.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=2) | Real imagery from three locations; 5,062 training images from seven ROS bags and 2,030 test images from two ROS bags. Labels support human detection and distance estimation. | **Out of scope:** no landing-pad target class; thesis page does not provide dataset reuse terms or a data download. |
| [Nested-marker landing guidance](https://www.mdpi.com/2504-446X/9/1/57) | Reports real DJI Tello experiments plus Gazebo simulation and a custom nested ArUco marker. The article lists 5,818 training and 443 test samples; its linked repository exposes code, not a verified public image dataset. | **Not admitted:** marker ontology differs; data provenance and a reusable annotated dataset are not established. |

No images or videos were downloaded from these sources, and no detector
inference was run. They do not change the frozen `NO_DATASET_ADMITTED` state.
