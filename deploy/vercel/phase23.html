Phase 23 · KIOS protected detector benchmark

The detector gained recall.

A 480px YOLO11n detector improved the clean and blur results over the earlier 320px detector. Occlusion and mixed stress got worse.

Detector comparison. The published Phase 23 results use 86 protected test frames from the 422-frame labeled real-video subset. This detector track is separate from the frozen Phase 1–22 simulation record.

Method and boundary

Phase 23 trained a detector; Phase 24 later reanalyzed its committed condition aggregates without new training.

Two KIOS detectors

The comparator is a 320px YOLO11n detector. Phase 23 used a 480px YOLO11n with UAV-oriented image augmentations. The source calls the comparator “Phase 22 Baseline”; it is distinct from the frozen Phase 22 simulation transfer model.

Temporal split

The 422 labeled frames were split into 252 train, 64 validation, 20 embargoed, and 86 protected test frames. Stress conditions reuse the protected test frames.

Limits: These are detector metrics on one protected dataset split. They do not demonstrate landing safety, controller behavior, certification, or real-flight readiness. safety_acceptance=false · controller_tuning_allowed=false.
