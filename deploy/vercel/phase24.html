How to read this

The Phase 24 result is a diagnostic summary of already-published detector metrics, not a new experiment.

Macro method

We take the unweighted mean across the six condition-level scores. The occlusion + mixed-stress view equally averages those two conditions, so it makes the tail visible rather than letting clean frames dominate.

One temporal holdout

The same 86-frame protected temporal test split is reused across conditions. The six stress views are not 516 independent examples. The dataset contains 422 labeled frames in total.

Limits: Per-frame predictions are not in the repository, so no confidence intervals or significance tests are reported. Detector precision, recall, and mAP do not demonstrate landing safety, controller performance, or real-flight readiness. safety_acceptance=false · controller_tuning_allowed=false.
