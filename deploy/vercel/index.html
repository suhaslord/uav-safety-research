Our goal

Make autonomous perception worthy of trust.

The project is not trying to make a landing demo look successful. It tests when a perception estimate should be trusted, challenged by independent evidence, widened with calibrated uncertainty, or rejected. The experiment record matters as much as the headline metric.

Independent evidenceA second signal should be useful without silently becoming the same failure mode.
Calibrated uncertaintyConfidence should survive distribution shift, not only development data.
Controlled abstentionRefusing a bad estimate can be safer, but excessive refusal is itself a failure.
