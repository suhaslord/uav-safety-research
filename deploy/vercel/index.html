Research question

When synthetic UAV landing perception becomes stale or degraded, what still tells us whether the estimate is actually reliable?

The study asks whether frozen uncertainty and local error-dynamics diagnostics can distinguish confidence from correctness, explain which synthetic contexts drive failure, and transfer those patterns without refitting.

UncertaintyDoes interval width still track actual estimation error under degradation?
Level errorHow much does a stale point estimate worsen absolute lateral accuracy and coverage?
Local dynamicsCan one-step residual structure remain predictable even when absolute error gets worse?

Main purpose

Build an inspectable research record, not a demo that only shows wins.

AegisLand separates uncertainty calibration, absolute estimation quality, failure attribution, and local one-step predictability. Candidates are frozen before downstream evidence, negative results remain visible, and later phases narrow the question instead of rewriting earlier failures.

Problem being studiedA system can look confident while its estimate is wrong.
Why that mattersInterval width, level error, and short-horizon residual dynamics do not have to move together.
Research disciplineDevelopment, transfer, protected validation, and final holdout are exposed sequentially rather than reused as one test set.

Research method

Freeze first. Expose evidence once.

The protocol is designed so a protected or final result cannot be tuned into a pass after it is seen. Later phases can ask a narrower new question, but they do not retroactively change an earlier verdict.

Lock the candidate.

Coefficients, thresholds, context definitions, and scientific code are frozen before downstream evidence is exposed.

01 · Freeze

Use fresh evidence roles.

Development, transfer, protected validation, and final holdout remain separate evidence stages.

02 · Separate

03 · Preserve

Main conclusion

Uncertainty, level error, and local predictability are different things.

Across the frozen simulation studies, pure two-frame stale estimates can worsen absolute lateral error while Phase 12 interval widths remain unchanged. A context-specific one-step residual structure can still be predictable, and later phases show that much of its synthetic context dependence is first-order and can be approximated additively on fresh 32-cell context cubes.

This does not mean latency is beneficial. Static latency-induced level-error degradation persists.

Phase 19 · ResidualsA narrower distribution-wide one-step residual advantage replicated in the simple synthetic context.
Phases 20–21 · StructureContext attenuation was distributed across predefined modifiers and was predominantly first-order.
