What we learned · what is next

Stop testing only easy failures.

H3's source/seed admission checker and read-only PX4 ULog signal preflight are now merged. No independent second-plant motor-fault dataset has been admitted or scored. The next scientific evidence must come from different dynamics and new test conditions.

01 / NEW PROTOCOL

Independent dynamics and fresh holdout

Reserve a new simulator, vehicle model or genuinely perturbed plant and unseen trajectories before evaluating the frozen rule.

02 / REALISTIC STRESS

Faults that develop gradually

Predeclare smaller partial losses, variable onset, online measurement noise, motion variation and clean healthy-control challenges.

03 / HONEST COMPARISON

Measure misses and false alarms

Compare with simple residual baselines. Track fault detection, incorrect motor identification, missed cases and response times without retuning on test outcomes.

Research boundary

The hardware-free experiments do not establish flight safety, controller recovery, a working physical UAV detector, or independent Phase 26 landing-pad validation. H2's “50% retention” means half commanded motor speed—not half thrust. Nothing here was connected to a flying aircraft.

Retained negative evidence

The first model wasn't reliable.

We kept the original exploratory result, rather than replacing it with the later successes.

49.1%

The initial 15-trajectory NumPy MLP pilot achieved only 49.1% held-out balanced accuracy on overlapping windows from one held-out seed. Some late motor-failure Webots trajectories also developed physically implausible dynamics. That motivated the corrected, early-window H1 protocol. This weak pilot was not retroactively relabeled as a successful detector.
