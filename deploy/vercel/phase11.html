Phase 11 · P14R · frozen historical result

High availability, good coverage, one tail-efficiency failure.

The failed gate

One locked tail metric stopped the phase.

Protected validation passed the required availability, coverage, calibration, rescue, and most efficiency checks. The lateral p95 interval-width / p95-error component did not. Its frozen maximum stayed 2.25× after the 2.435× result was visible.

The final P15-v2 holdout was not opened after this failure. Any later work had to start as a new preregistered phase with fresh evidence.
