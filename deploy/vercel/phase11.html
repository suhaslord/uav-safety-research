What changed

Three bounded changes define P14R.

The primary estimator remains bounded. Rescue stays independent. Uncertainty transfer becomes robust across grouped compound shift.

Bounded primary path

The trusted continuity estimator remains capped and nonrecursive rather than turning successful rescue into future primary state.

Independent rescue

Primary-unavailable truth-visible observations can be recovered without allowing the rescue path to silently become the new anchor.

Robust uncertainty envelope

Groupwise conformal uncertainty is calibrated for compound shift instead of depending on one fragile severity partition.
