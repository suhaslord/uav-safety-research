Recovery and regression on reused frames

Descriptive comparison across 86 frames and 6 conditions from two video sequences.

  • clean: baseline 53/86, Phase 23 46/86; 7 recovered, 14 regressed.

These repeated views describe the observed frames. Training-resolution and central-ring explanations remain untested hypotheses; the archived significance and uncertainty claims are superseded.

05 / Corrected exploratory topology analysis

5,160 archived treatment views

Post hoc descriptive correction of CENTER, OUTER_RING, STRIPED and RANDOM_PATCH observations.

  • Matched achieved-dose bins: OUTER_RING minus STRIPED: +5.60 percentage points across 86 frames and 633 shared frame/bin pairs. Rates are compared within each frame and shared bin, then averaged equally by frame. This is an exploratory contrast.
  • Observed crossing: OUTER_RING success is 101/214 (47.20%) in achieved-dose bin [0.05, 0.15). Its first downward 50% crossing interpolates to 0.072282; the curve is non-monotonic.
  • Common support: 13 views at achieved doses of 0.75 or more are excluded. The corrected summary records denominators, dose distributions and sequence strata.
  • Historical control limitation: JPEG reencoding changed zero-dose image bytes and false-positive counts. The original aggregate gate evaluated source clean images. Lossless v2 inference has not been run.

Two source sequences do not support the archived confirmatory claims. These synthetic masks describe benchmark sensitivity without establishing internal detector mechanisms.

Epistemic Limits: Evaluated on 86 static KIOS test frames from 2 video sessions under synthetic neutral-gray occlusion masks. These results demonstrate pattern-specific detector sensitivity on a static image benchmark and do not establish flight safety, airworthiness, or real-world operational certification.
