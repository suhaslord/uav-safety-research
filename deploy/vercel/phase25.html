04 / Paired Failure-Mechanism Study

Why robust training helps blur/noise but degrades occlusion/mixed

Paired comparison of Phase 22 baseline vs authenticated Phase 23 robust detector across identical 86 frames × 6 conditions (516 paired views).

Frame-level Paired Outcomes: 49 RECOVERED, 75 REGRESSED, 223 BOTH PASS, 169 BOTH FAIL across 516 views.

Statistically Associated Properties:

  • Object Size (MWU p = 3.29×10−5): Smaller pads (Q1/Q2) suffer severe regression under occlusion due to the fixed 8px occluder clamp covering 60–100% of distant pads.
  • Confidence Shift (Wilcoxon p = 5.81×10−4): True positive scores collapse below false positive background contours under occlusion and mixed stress.
  • Error Dynamics (FP MWU p = 2.57×10−7, FN MWU p = 2.24×10−26): Robust training eliminates false negatives under benign blur/noise, but triggers false positives and misses under occluded center markings.

Root Cause: 480px training (2.25× pixel area) resolves fine concentric rings, delivering high blur/noise robustness. However, 55% central occlusion destroys these learned internal rings, while compound mixed degradation eliminates remaining perimeter gradients, causing Phase 23 recall to plummet to 8.1%.

Detector box correctness cannot establish landing safety or controller performance. No cutoff will be selected from this reused test set.
