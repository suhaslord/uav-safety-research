04 / Paired Failure-Mechanism Study

Why robust training helps blur/noise but degrades occlusion/mixed

Paired comparison of Phase 22 baseline vs authenticated Phase 23 robust detector across identical 86 frames × 6 conditions (516 paired views).

Frame-level Paired Outcomes: 49 RECOVERED, 65 REGRESSED, 223 BOTH PASS, 179 BOTH FAIL across 516 views.

Statistically Associated Properties:

  • Object Size (MWU p = 3.29×10−5): Smaller pads (Q1/Q2) suffer severe regression under occlusion due to the fixed 8px occluder clamp covering 60–100% of distant pads.
  • Confidence Shift (Wilcoxon p = 5.81×10−4): True positive scores collapse below false positive background contours under occlusion and mixed stress.
  • Error Dynamics (FP MWU p = 2.57×10−7, FN MWU p = 2.24×10−26): Robust training eliminates false negatives under benign blur/noise, but triggers false positives and misses under occluded center markings.

Root Cause Hypothesis: 480px training (2.25× pixel area) resolves fine concentric rings, delivering high blur/noise robustness. However, central occlusion disrupts these learned internal features, while compound mixed degradation eliminates remaining perimeter gradients, causing Phase 23 recall to plummet to 8.1%.

05 / Controlled Occlusion Topology Experiment

5,160 Treatment Views: Spatial Masking Topology Analysis

Pre-registered experimental evaluation of 86 source frames across 4 topologies (CENTER, OUTER_RING, STRIPED, RANDOM_PATCH) and 15 dose levels.

Multiplicity-Adjusted Confirmatory Finding: Under the pre-registered Bonferroni family-wise threshold (αadj = 0.05 / 6 = 0.00833):

  • OUTER_RING vs STRIPED: Statistically significant (Δ = +5.24 percentage points [95% CI: +1.55, +9.15], p = 0.002). Periphery-focused masking reliably outperforms alternating-stripe disruption.
  • OUTER_RING vs RANDOM_PATCH: Nominal association (Δ = +2.82 pp [95% CI: +0.39, +5.27], p = 0.010); does not meet the multiplicity-adjusted threshold.
  • CENTER vs OUTER_RING: Overall difference is not statistically significant (Δ = −2.39 pp [95% CI: −4.96, +0.08], p = 0.062).
  • Center-Sensitivity Verdict: Inconclusive / Suggestive. On distant/small targets at severe doses (≥0.50, N=66), an observed descriptive difference of 9.28 pp was noted (34.1% vs 43.4%, nominal p = 0.012), but does not meet the family-wise adjusted threshold.
  • Monotonicity Observation: Empirical binned success rates exhibit non-monotonic fluctuations due to discrete image composition; overall downward trends appear primarily in fitted regression models.
